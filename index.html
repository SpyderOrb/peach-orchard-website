01 / THE PLOT

Good fruit has nowhere to hide.

Our fertile ground and their daily work meet in a peach that is sweet, fragrant, and alive.

The orchard sits close to the family home in Kherson Oblast, where fertile ground meets the wide, open southern steppe. Small enough to care for closely, large enough to keep two brothers busy every day.

02 / THE SEASON

Care, repeated
until it tastes right.

  1. 01

    Fertilize with purpose

    Good fruit starts below the surface. The soil and every tree are fed at the right stage, giving roots the nutrients they need without wasting what the land provides.

  2. 02

    Protect the trees

    Disease and pests never take a season off. The orchard is watched constantly, then treated with the right crop protection at the right moment — carefully measured and never used blindly.

  3. 03

    Watch the water

    Heat changes quickly here. Irrigation is checked, adjusted, and checked again so the roots receive what they need, when they need it.

  4. 04

    Prune & thin

    Branches are opened to light and crowded fruit is thinned by hand. Fewer peaches on the tree means more strength, sweetness, and size in each one.

  5. 05

    Pick by hand

    Color is only the first clue. A peach is ready when it gives just enough beneath your thumb.

  6. 06

    Read the weather

    Forecasts, wind, heat, and humidity shape each day’s work. Plans change early, before the open steppe weather has a chance to do harm.

FIELD NOTES / A YEAR IN THE ORCHARD

Every peach has a before.

Four chapters, one tree. Follow the quiet work that leads to a full basket.

From the orchard notebook
Choose a chapter of the orchard year

01 / THE FIRST PROMISE

Before the fruit, a flower.

The branches begin again in pink. The basket is still a long way off; for now, the work is to watch the trees and keep an eye on the changing weather.

In the notebook: flowers, forecasts, a fresh start.

02 / THE EVERYDAY WORK

Room to grow. Time to care.

Leaves fill the branches and small green peaches take shape. Water is checked, crowded fruit is thinned, and the brothers return to the same rows, day after day.

In the notebook: water, space, patience.

03 / THE FULL BASKET

Picked when the peach says so.

Color, fragrance, a little give beneath the fingers. The fruit sets the pace, and picking happens by hand. This is the chapter you can taste.

In the notebook: gentle hands, full baskets, juice on your chin.

04 / THE QUIET CHAPTER

An empty branch is a beginning.

The baskets are put away and the orchard grows quiet. There are tools to mend and notes to revisit before the story begins again with a flower.

In the notebook: repairs, reflection, next year.
