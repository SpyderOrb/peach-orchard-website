# Image generation prompts

Both final images were generated with the built-in image generation tool and then optimized to WebP for the site.

## Orchard hero

**Use case:** photorealistic-natural  
**Asset type:** wide landing-page hero photograph for a small family peach orchard  
**Primary request:** Create a completely new, highly believable editorial photograph inspired by the provided real orchard references. Show a small, honestly worked peach orchard in southern Moldova / Eastern European countryside at late golden hour: low rows of mature peach trees visibly heavy with ripe yellow-red peaches, dry fertile dark earth between rows, practical irrigation hoses, and the modest working base near the orchard edge with a simple weathered shed, covered agricultural sprayer and stacked utility barrels. It must feel like a real family place next to a home, not a resort and not an industrial plantation.  
**Input images:** Orchard video contact sheets are environment and color references only; the bitten peach photo is a texture/color reference only.  
**Style/medium:** Photorealistic documentary photography, 35mm lens, realistic scale, gentle fine grain, imperfect natural detail.  
**Composition/framing:** Horizontal composition, view down one orchard row, tree leaves framing the upper edge, closest ripe peaches large enough to feel tactile, the humble base visible but secondary in the middle distance; calm darker foliage space for light headline text.  
**Lighting/mood:** Warm late-afternoon sun after work, sun streaks through leaves, honest, intimate, abundant but not luxurious.  
**Palette:** Deep leaf green, sun-baked umber, peach yellow, coral blush, muted shed green, warm cream highlights.  
**Constraints:** No people, text, logos, perfect symmetry, tropical scenery, mountains, giant farm buildings or watermark. Keep it recognizably small-scale and utilitarian.

## Igor and Vitalii

**Use case:** photorealistic-natural  
**Asset type:** editorial founder portrait for a family peach orchard website  
**Primary request:** Create a new photorealistic documentary-style photograph of the two real men from the reference portraits standing together in their small peach orchard, shaking hands naturally at chest/waist height, shoulders turned slightly toward one another, and both looking directly at the camera with relaxed, proud expressions. Preserve each man's recognizable identity closely: Igor is the older man with narrow face, moustache/goatee and cap; Vitalii is the smiling younger man with short dark hair and clean-shaven face. Put Igor in a faded olive work overshirt over a plain light tee, and Vitalii in a dusty blue work shirt with no logo or text.  
**Input images:** Igor and Vitalii portraits are identity references; orchard frames are environment references only.  
**Scene/backdrop:** A real small Eastern European peach orchard with mature narrow-leaf trees, visible ripe yellow-red peaches, dry worked earth and an irrigation hose softly out of focus.  
**Style/medium:** Highly realistic candid editorial photography, natural skin texture, believable hands and clothing wear, 50mm lens, shallow but not extreme depth of field, gentle film grain.  
**Composition/framing:** Horizontal waist-up portrait, handshake fully visible and anatomically correct, both faces unobstructed, equal visual importance.  
**Lighting/mood:** Late-afternoon sunlight filtered through leaves; warm, honest, quiet pride after a day of work.  
**Constraints:** Exactly two men, preserve identities and approximate ages, both looking at camera, one natural handshake, no logos, text, extra limbs, cloned faces, suits, corporate setting or watermark.
